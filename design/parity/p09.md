## Evidence for (b): the share review in the design

Question: does the Figma share review list all shareable saved properties with checkboxes, or only the comparison selection, and what is preselected?

Answer: neither. The design review lists **no per-property control at all**. It is a read-only summary of a fixed pair, **№202 and №200**, which are also the two properties on P09 and the first two of the three on P08.

1. X11 `20:3192` (desktop), `23:5187` (mobile) and prototype `63:29250` hold exactly this content: H1 «Споделете само публичното»; a sentence «Преглед за създателя · Включени обяви №202 и №200. Лични имена, критерии, бюджет, бележки и съобщения са изключени.» (`20:3205`); two rows, «Срок — 7 дни от потвърденото създаване» and «Текущ линк — Още няма активна връзка»; buttons «Създайте връзка за тези обяви» (primary) and «Отказ».

3. The two ids repeat in X11S («Само обяви №202 и №200», `22:1831`), X11M («Публични обяви — №202 · №200», `20:3273`), X11CONF (`45:4092`) and the P09 body (`13:579`, `13:591`). Property №912 (`11:1321`, the third saved property on P08) is never included.

P08's cards show only «Запазен имот» and «Сравни»; the design draws no per-card selection state, so how «избраните» in «Споделете избраните имоти» (`I11:1332;6:6`) gets chosen is undefined in Figma. Code, for contrast: the review lists every shareable saved property (up to 12) with an «Включи във връзката» checkbox; all are included until the person unchecks (`share-panel.tsx:84`; `e2e/shared-shortlist.spec.ts:219` expects «3 / 12» before it unchecks the third). Recommendation: keep the checkboxes (they give G3.4 control the design lacks), seed them from the comparison selection when one exists (P08-02), and ask design to draw the checkbox review frame and the selection step (G-01).
