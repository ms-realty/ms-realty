## Authority and invariants

Use `docs/architecture.md`, ADR 0002 and `docs/ux-spec.md` for the rebuild implementation. `production/data/launch-readiness.json` and `production/data/launch-input-checklist.md` remain the current launch authority, preserved verbatim from the primary checkout. Their historical pass entries do not establish a fresh rebuild pass, and their conflicting runtime obligations cannot silently disappear. Until the owner records the successor launch policy, R00 remains blocked. The supplied owner decision makes Search Console, Yandex Webmaster and backlink exports optional at all stages. Exact URL dispositions and crawl parity for both domains remain mandatory.

BG remains the source locale. Translation drafting never grants indexability. Keep original property facts, provenance and revision identity. Staff and clients use separate hosts, cookies and WebAuthn relying parties. Every sensitive command rechecks authorization and expected revision. Hermes cannot publish, send, approve, index or grant access. Production data and provider accounts are never substituted with fixture success.

## Work ownership and review

The integration owner controls schema, dependency lockfile, host routing, shared catalogs, CI and final checks. Identity, form patterns and release/transport tooling can be repaired independently on disjoint files. Subsequent work starts only when its shared contracts are stable. Every handoff names its source, write scope, tests and remaining limitations. Independent review follows integration; a worker's summary alone does not close a gate.

## Agency inputs and final release boundary

Owner inputs remain explicit: approved legal/contact/service and retention policy; named staffing and hours; locale reviewers; seller/media authority per listing; legal/accounting review of transaction templates; agency-owned provider accounts; DNS and domain renewal; signing/recovery custody; actual human listing review; and candidate/cutover acceptance. Missing inputs block their dependent live capabilities rather than being replaced with invented values.

The implementation can progress locally while these are outstanding. A reproducible local build, synthetic database, screenshot or green CI cannot establish live provider delivery, human approval, recovery readiness, production deployment or operational acceptance.
