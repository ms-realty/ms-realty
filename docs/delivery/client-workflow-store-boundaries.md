# C05/C06 private workflow stores

These source classifications describe local controls. They establish no operator policy acceptance, independent archive state, live recovery result or production qualification.

| Store | Ownership and permitted readback | Export and source binding | Erasure and recovery boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `appointment_participant_responses` | The actual entitled client records their own answer to an immutable proposed version, slot and timezone. An answer grants no access and confirms no booking. Clients see only their own answer; current appointment staff may coordinate through the appointment. | Only the subject's authored decision/text and schedule metadata enter their own dossier. Text can be redacted before disclosure. Raw operation/grant payloads and other participants' responses are absent. | Retained appointment business evidence. The principal foreign key participates in the conservative dependency/retention hold. This is outside optional-copy erasure; no appointment/history deletion is claimed. |
| `personal_saved_listings` | Current invited-client identity with current Case eligibility owns each personal save. Only currently approved public presentation is resolved; a missing presentation remains an unavailable reference. No Case Interest or grant is created. | Own saved references and creation instants enter the dossier and source digest. | Explicit per-source human classification may erase optional saved copies through `client_profile_copies`, subject to existing policy, retention and business-dependency guards. The safety ledger stores exact saved IDs and reapplies their removal after a restore. Other principals' saves remain outside the effect. |
| `saved_selection_transfers` | A public creator session explicitly submits at most 100 selected references. The nonce is bound to exact source Origin, client target and a 30-minute validity window. It travels in a top-level POST body and a host-only continuation cookie. A GET does not merge. Confirmation atomically binds it to one eligible client and unions saves. | Consumed own reference selections and creation/consumption instants enter the own dossier. Raw nonce, nonce hash and public creator-session hash never enter that dossier. Unconsumed transfers have no verified person link. | Expired unconsumed selections are invalid and are pruned when the same creator prepares another transfer. Consumed selections are retained for the durable same-owner merge result until an authorized optional-copy erasure; their exact IDs enter restore reapplication. This does not claim a scheduled physical purge or independent archive deletion. |
| `inquiry_attachments` | Receipt-session-bound owner-inquiry evidence; no Property, Case, public-media or usage-right grant follows from upload. Private object bytes remain behind scan and authorization checks. | The public worker's allowlisted metadata/source projection enters the own dossier and private-file coverage. Changed scan/content commitments invalidate review. Unsafe or unauthorized bytes block disclosure. Object keys, filenames and receipt secrets remain absent. | Retained inquiry business evidence. The explicit inquiry-attachment lock and opaque revision enter erasure dependency binding. Physical disposal stays held for a separately accepted, verified object-erasure policy. Existing recovery quarantine remains authoritative. |

The accepted currency schema currently allows BGN, EUR, GBP and USD, all with two minor digits. Owner-preview and saved-criteria readback use the shared formatter. Zero- or three-minor-digit currency support is not asserted by this source change. The existing non-EUR saved-search editing guard remains in force.

Closed client Cases show an honest absence of a shared remaining-obligation record and an accountable support path. Internal closeout evidence is not republished as client-safe aftercare. Granular event-by-channel preference rules and unrestricted public subscriber enrollment remain separate policy/schema inputs; interface copy does not create those authorities.
