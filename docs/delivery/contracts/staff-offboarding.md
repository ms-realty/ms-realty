# O23 — staff access removal and retained handover

A current staff access manager with two active passkeys and a verification at most five
minutes old may end another staff member's membership. Self-removal is refused. Record-scoped
access grants cannot authorize this account-wide action. A reviewed reason/handover plan is
required; authentication expiry returns to a deliberate review rather than automatically
executing a previous request.

The command shares the capability-management transaction lock and locks staff principals in
the established order. It rechecks the operator, expected principal revision and active
membership, then ends membership, revokes all staff sessions/passkeys/pending staff invitations
and explicit grants. A usable access manager must remain. An identical operation replay
returns the same receipt; competing removals cannot disable both operators. Historical identity,
records, grants and their audit trail are retained. Future re-employment uses a new explicit
staff invitation and new factors; previous grants do not reactivate.

Physical keys are not returned by access removal. Cases, tasks and inquiries are not closed,
reassigned or falsely completed. The page shows bounded handover counts before and after
removal, and the receipt records the counts observed by the transaction. Those counts are an
operational snapshot, not a freeze of future work. Custody commands share-lock an eligible
holder while recording issue/deadline changes, so an offboarding change cannot race past a
stale eligibility check. A custody manager may record actual return after offboarding.

The access page links active staff to the review and the most recent 50 former staff to retained
handover. A known direct staff URL remains available to an authorized manager. Detailed key
records still require key.manage; account access managers see only the count. The page does not
show private Case/task/inquiry contents or raw physical storage data through these counts.

Forms work with and without JavaScript and retain rejected reason text while clearing the
confirmation. Successful actor-bound receipts survive reload. Unknown or mismatched receipts
cannot assert completion or offer a replacement destructive form. No external mail/provider
operation is initiated. Pending invitation dispatch/redeem and consequential queued actions
retain their existing live authority checks.

This closes application access removal and key-custody continuity. Assignment-by-assignment
handover, device/session inventory outside this application, payroll/HR and provider-account
removal remain separate human/operator work; the software does not claim those were completed.
