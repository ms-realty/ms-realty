# O24 operating-policy source contract

The agency settings route is `/{locale}/settings/operating-policy`; personal preferences remain separate. The fixed `operating_policy` resource uses ID `00000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000024`. Ordinary `settings.manage` grants apply to that resource, including scoped grants. Draft saving and approval recheck live staff membership and passkey enrollment. Approval additionally requires fresh authentication, explicit human review, an evidence note, the exact draft revision and its content hash.

Drafts are editable. Approved editions, their audit events and operation receipts are durable. Same-payload replay returns the original result; changed payloads, stale revisions and stale hashes fail. Approval does not send a customer message, approve a provider, ratify a legal claim or clear a launch gate. BG, EN and RU interface copy remains covered by the staff catalogs' draft review status.

The reviewed Sofia calendar records validity bounds, explicit closed/unknown/staffed weekdays, multiple non-overlapping intervals and holiday exceptions. A missing calendar, unknown day or interval outside its reviewed bounds cannot establish staffed duration or authorize a new viewing under that calendar. Legacy service-policy rows retain their existing safety-guard behavior and are never promoted into reviewed operating-policy editions.

New inquiries and appointment requests store an immutable copy of the approved policy, or an explicit unknown state. First appointment confirmation separately records the actual policy used by its service-hours guard. Policy edits never rewrite those contexts. Appointment history includes both snapshots. Historical unbound rows stay unbound. Intake still accepts a durable request without inventing a response guarantee; production capture remains subject to the independent launch and privacy gates.

New human listing-availability confirmations adopt the currently reviewed interval. Approved intervals may shorten the architecture's sale/rental baseline. An explicitly unknown interval holds the new confirmation. The confirmation stores its policy context and exact `reviewDueAt`, with the same values in immutable audit. Search and public presentation read that recorded horizon. Policy changes do not extend or shorten earlier evidence. Legacy unbound contexts retain the existing baseline without gaining a human approval. Existing subscription, privacy and inventory controls remain separately permissioned and linked where authorized.

For B07, `staffedDuration(snapshot, start, end)` in `src/domain/operating-policy.ts` returns known elapsed milliseconds with policy ID/version, or an explicit unknown reason. It excludes closed intervals, applies date exceptions, counts real elapsed time across Sofia DST and bounds calculation to 366 elapsed days. Callers must preserve the unknown denominator. `firstResponseAt` continues to mean the first recorded human response; automatic acknowledgment is separate. No SLA threshold or public guarantee is inferred.

Additive SQL31 creates the draft/edition tables, protects approved projections and accepted context, and adds the listing confirmation binding. Canonical migration journal/snapshot integration follows SQL30. Synthetic unit, database and browser fixtures establish source behavior only. Actual staffing, named ownership, policy sources, provider review and human acceptance still require real operator decisions.
