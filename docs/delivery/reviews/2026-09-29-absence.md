# O23 / UX18: planned absence and explicit return

Architecture §6.6 requires coverage when a staff member is absent, without losing commitments.
An active membership can now carry a start instant and a required coverage-review deadline.
The start may be immediate or scheduled. At that instant, existing Cases, tasks, inquiries and
physical-key recovery work derive coverage from the same membership record. No worker or
bulk ownership rewrite is involved. The deadline prompts review; it never infers a return.

An access manager confirms return or cancels a scheduled absence after reviewing retained
work. Records still assigned to that person resume normal responsibility; accepted handovers
remain with their new owners. Absence does not revoke sessions, passkeys or permissions.
Physical custody, deadlines and client promises stay recorded exactly as before.

The command requires live staff authority, two passkeys, fresh reauthentication, a reviewed
plan, an operation identifier and the expected membership revision. Its audit and receipt are
atomic with the change. Duplicate submissions replay the receipt. Competing changes require
explicit review. A failed transaction leaves availability and effective coverage unchanged.
Scheduling absence, revoking grants and offboarding retain a usable manager without a pending
absence. A pending absence has no automatic end, so it cannot stand in for lasting management
coverage. Calendar staffing policy, business hours and the agency's actual rota remain inputs.

Task and Case receivers, inquiry acceptance, new Case creation and key-holder eligibility
check current availability. Confirmation of an appointment also checks a planned host absence
through the end of the slot and its travel buffer. These checks retain existing authorization and lock responsibility
changes against concurrent absence administration. Viewing records and requesting help to
hand over existing work remain available to the absent employee.

The BG/RU/EN management screen links to the absence workflow, displays planned/current absence
and overdue review, and lists only retained records the manager is allowed to read. The native
form preserves a conflicting draft, clears confirmation and provides operation reconciliation
after navigation or a lost response. Staff translations remain subject to human language review.
