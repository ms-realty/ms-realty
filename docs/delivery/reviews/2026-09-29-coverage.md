# Effective coverage after staff revocation

Architecture §6.6 requires active work to remain accountable when a staff member loses access.
The prior offboarding workflow retained records and enabled individual handovers, but those
records still appeared assigned to an active colleague and had no shared recovery queue.

Open Cases, tasks and inquiries now enter the agency coverage queue as soon as their named
owner has no active staff principal/membership. This is a projection of the committed identity
state, so it takes effect in the same administrative transaction, rolls back with it and has no
background worker delay. Offboarding's audit and durable receipt name the agency queue.
The last accepted owner remains recorded; requesting a new owner does not remove work from
coverage. The named receiver's acceptance does. Closed records stay out of the queue.

Today, record ownership labels and offboarding link to the paginated BG/RU/EN queue. Record
permissions are applied before each category's limit. Access administration alone does not
reveal private Cases, tasks or inquiries. Physical keys appear as recovery work with their
actual recorded holder; revocation never fabricates a return or custody transfer. Promises,
deadlines, dependencies and parent records survive a task handover unchanged.

The projection covers ended/suspended membership and suspended principals. Planned absence
needs a separate, explicit administrative workflow; it is not inferred from calendar events or
sign-in inactivity. No staffing rota, automated notification or automatic bulk transfer is claimed.
Case/inquiry/key end-to-end handover qualification remains distinct from task acceptance.
