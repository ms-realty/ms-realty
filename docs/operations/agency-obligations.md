# B10 agency obligations: controlled manual procedure

This is an exercise procedure and evidence-packet contract for OPS09, OPS05 and STA09,
under plan §7. It supplies no real partner terms, reporting interval, property access,
media license, review policy or customer consent. Local synthetic receipts establish only
their tested software behavior. Live services, professional review, provider delivery,
human acceptance and R00–R12 release evidence remain separate.

## Partner and referral terms

Open **Agency obligations → Partners and referrals**. A standing relationship uses the
counterparty's currently permitted contact record; it requires no consumer Case. A real
Case may be linked when relevant. Keep the relationship, accountable staff owner, actual
purpose, supplied terms text, source reference and actual version together. An optional
document binds the exact current sealed, clean-scanned, purpose-reviewed version and
digest under its own restricted-document permission.

Transcribe actual supplied terms. Record unknown fee basis, payer, trigger, disclosure or
policy questions explicitly. Leave acceptance and expiry unknown until actual evidence is
available. A draft is not counterparty acceptance. An individually qualified human with
current `compliance.review` and `claim.approve` records the exact acceptance only after
reviewing the source, actual counterparty acceptance reference, effective acceptance time,
actual expiry and responsible qualified person. The same qualified person may own and
review the record with one active passkey. No separate person's account is required.

Saving the source explicitly makes the signed-in person its accountable owner. Owner identity
is part of the exact terms binding; an unavailable owner holds positive acceptance.
Source edits create a new draft and clear approval. Replaced or revoked documents, expired
terms and revoked qualification make current eligibility false. Original text, acceptance,
source version, actor and operation remain in private immutable history under their original
Case, counterparty and document permissions. There is no invoice, settlement or commission
calculation in this record.

After an identity merge, positive use and acceptance of the original counterparty remain
held. Qualified retained reads and revocation use the still-permitted original Case,
document or original contact path; they never substitute a canonical party. A standing
text-only source with no preserved ordinary bridge requires an explicit B04 merge hold and
manual decision before merging. Retained evidence alone grants no new customer authority.

## Human-approved owner report

Use the existing Case workspace, messages/email and owned task controls. Before the
exercise, the owner supplies the actual reporting interval, report period, responsible
person, permitted audience and channel. Without those inputs, retain an owned manual
clarification task; neither a report count nor a completed generic task satisfies this duty.

1. Read the current permitted Case, Listing revision, publication/availability state and
   source-backed facts. Preserve price, area, bedrooms, location, reference and source URL
   exactly. Distinguish activity from outcomes and retain unknown delivery or customer
   responses as unknown.
2. Prepare the exact report for the supplied period. Butler may draft only inside the
   selected task from permitted sources. A person reviews the source, draft and audience.
3. Use the in-app Case message only when every current Case participant is an intended
   recipient. Otherwise use the existing email draft and an explicit currently eligible
   recipient. Do not widen Case participation to send the report.
4. Record the existing exact-body/recipient/channel `message_send` approval. For email,
   retain draft version, review hash, operation and provider outcome separately. A queued
   email, provider acceptance and delivered email are different states.
5. Keep the report's message ID, approved version/hash, source/period, accountable owner,
   audience, human approval and receipt. Record the next owned reporting commitment using
   the actual agreed interval or an explicit waiting dependency. Do not invent a schedule.

The exercise packet contains those references plus the actual observed transport result and
the next permitted manual action. It contains no customer text, contacts, access codes or
other personal data in committed evidence. An internal draft or synthetic fixture is not an
owner-approved report.

## Photography and media rights

Use **Listing → More → Property photography**. Record the actual photography scope and
access dependency. This creates property work with no buyer Case or Interest. The existing
appointment workbench proposes, confirms, reschedules, cancels and records outcomes using
Sofia time and explicit DST handling. Confirmation rechecks the current host, permitted
property/listing, manual access confirmation, external-busy check, approved service hours,
travel buffers and exclusive host/property intervals. A proposed replacement leaves the
committed interval in force. Host handover retains the old host until the new eligible host
personally accepts. ICS export is a participant-scoped calendar artifact, not attendance.

Appointment completion records only its factual outcome. It does not create or approve an
asset, rights or publication. Supply the actual rights provenance before the separate media
exercise:

1. Upload the actual bytes through the existing private media path; retain the server-sealed
   digest, clean scan, derivative results and current asset version.
2. A person reviews the actual rights holder/reference, usage scope and privacy against the
   exact asset. Missing or changed rights remain held. A photography appointment, custody
   record or complaint outcome is not a license.
3. Keep rights review, photo draft, Listing placement, editorial decisions and publication
   manifest separate. Exercise the actual public rendition only after the required current
   decisions and provider checks exist.

The media packet retains asset/version/digest, actual supplied rights reference, human scope,
scan/derivative results, placement and publication receipt separately. Source controls alone
do not prove live storage/scanner/publication behavior or physical access.

## Completed-Case review consent and verification

Prepare a Bulgarian CMS **Review** with the exact consented public name (or anonymous
heading), review text as the first paragraph, and the actual verification statement as the
second. Complete qualified source-claim review, then bind that exact edition to the
completed Case and its actual eligible author in **Agency obligations → Case reviews**.
There is no generic “verified customer” assertion. Customer contact details are excluded.

The verified original author can grant separate publication consent in the client review
page under the current approved `review-publication-consent` policy. They can refuse or
withdraw without a current positive policy or continuing Case access. Staff can instead
record an externally supplied consent/refusal/withdrawal reference with the actual reviewed
`review_consent` Case document. Service or marketing consent cannot substitute.

Positive consent stays held when the policy, completed Case, eligible author or exact wording
is missing. An individually qualified human verifies the Case, author, exact assent and
verification statement. Existing CMS editorial and publication decisions remain separate.
Publication cannot succeed through a generic CMS approval without the live review binding.
The supported display is Bulgarian; another language requires its own wording consent and
qualified language/publication path before it can display.

Wording replacement resets consent and verification. Refusal or withdrawal restricts the
origin page and dependent locale editions immediately in the same transaction, preserving
immutable past wording. Every public origin read also rechecks the original author's durable
privacy restriction and current evidence; it never transfers consent to a merged identity.
This does not establish an external cache purge. Any provider cache/crawl exercise remains
part of exact-release live qualification.

Retain review/Case/original-author identity, exact edition/hash, public name choice, separate
consent source/policy/version, refusal/withdrawal history, qualified verification, CMS
decisions, operation receipts and observed origin/provider behavior in the private packet.
The approved actual review policy, real consent and human acceptance remain operator inputs.

## Unknown outcomes

Use the exact action-status link before retrying. Receipt reads are actor, command, record
and key bound and recheck current ordinary permissions. An unresolved command on that
record cannot be bypassed by submitting a different key. Reconcile the original operation
or use the approved manual recovery path; do not manufacture a positive result.
